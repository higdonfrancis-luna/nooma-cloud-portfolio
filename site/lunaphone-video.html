A phone agent on Azure

A 9-minute walkthrough of how LunaPhone, an AI phone agent, was built: Twilio on the phone line, a Python service on Azure App Service, and the Azure OpenAI Realtime API, plus the real problems worked through along the way.

Covered in the video:

  • Twilio webhooks, TwiML and Media Streams over a WebSocket
  • Azure App Service on Linux: Python 3.11, Oryx builds and Gunicorn
  • Flask and Flask-Sock bridging two WebSockets per call
  • The Azure OpenAI Realtime API: one model that hears, reasons and speaks
  • Real lessons: Azure authentication, phone audio formats, startup and pickup speed
